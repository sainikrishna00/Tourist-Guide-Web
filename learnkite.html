Kite festival

The annual Jodhpur International Desert Kite Festival in Rajasthan, India, provides the exact experience of joining local celebrations and learning traditional kite-making, generally held every January.

The Jodhpur International Desert Kite Festival

This vibrant three-day festival takes place annually around Makar Sankranti (January 14th), celebrating the transition from winter to summer and the approaching harvest season. The main events are held at the Polo Ground in Jodhpur.

Key Experiences:

1. Join local celebrations:

The festival is a huge cultural event that brings together locals, national, and international participants. The atmosphere is lively, filled with music, food stalls, and general festive cheer.

2. Witness kite-making:

While the main event is the flying competition, the surrounding markets and cultural displays often feature local artisans and offer opportunities to see traditional kite-making (known as patang baazi). The nearby city of Ahmedabad is particularly famous for its kite-making communities.

3. Kite flying:

The sky is a canvas of thousands of colorful kites. You can bring your own or buy one at the venue and join in the friendly competitions. Helicopters have even been known to release large numbers of kites into the sky to mark the occasion.

4. Cultural immersion:

Beyond the kites, the festival offers a deep dive into Rajasthani culture with traditional music and dance performances, local delicacies like Undhiyu and Jalebi, and stalls selling local handicrafts. This festival provides a perfect opportunity to immerse yourself in local traditions and appreciate the craftsmanship involved in traditional kite making.
